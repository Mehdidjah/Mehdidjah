The stack layout is a custom SVG composition inspired by the supplied reference
from [hexxt.dev](https://hexxt.dev/).

Brand logos come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons),
distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
The Chargily symbol comes from the reference site's
[Chargily SVG](https://hexxt.dev/technologies/chargily.svg).
Logos identify their respective technologies; all marks belong to their owners.

Exact source URLs and embedded vectors are in `scripts/tech-stack-icons.json`.
Run `python3 scripts/build-tech-stack.py` to rebuild the six responsive variants.
